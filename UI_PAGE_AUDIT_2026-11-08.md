# WIRE TO WIRE — Page-by-Page UI Audit
**Date:** 2026-11-08
**Scope:** Every route and shared surface in the current archive app. Visual direction: premium Thoroughbred operations desk with deep forest, ivory, restrained brass and the existing red brand accent. Fictional artwork remains placeholder-only.

| Page or surface | Audit finding | Update |
|---|---|---|
| Command Center | Strong dashboard structure, but the role controls could read as an active simulation. | Increased hierarchy and contrast; labeled role and involvement as future game preferences; stated that simulation is paused. |
| Race Calendar | A long vertical race list is not a calendar and hides the shape of a season. | Replaced it with an interactive Sunday-first month grid, month picker, previous/next controls, monthly race card, and separate panels for undated, held, and not-run entries. |
| My Stable | Cards need clear identity, source class, and compact performance facts. | Kept image placeholders, emphasized horse names and recorded stakes facts, and gave the roster cards stronger depth and spacing. |
| People & Farms | People and organizations were combined into one undifferentiated grid. | Split into clearly counted People and Farms & Organizations sections; kept portrait/logo slots as placeholders. |
| Racecourses | The registry is inherently tabular, but the current treatment feels like a raw database. | Added a high-contrast table header, row striping, hover state, and clearer record status hierarchy. |
| History Vault | Race records need fast scanning by time, with confidence visible. | Preserved chronological year/month grouping, added date-evidence badges, and styled the archive rows as compact fixtures. |
| Record Review | Proposal governance is important but visually blends into ordinary rows. | Strengthened status, evidence, and action hierarchy while preserving proposal-only behavior and source protection. |
| History Coverage | Reconciliation detail was visually dense and easy to miss. | Made the source reconciliation panel high-contrast and elevated the delta and certification metrics. |
| Search | Race matches were not chronological and result cards had weak hierarchy. | Sorted race matches by year/date/name and polished result-card hierarchy while preserving identity IDs. |
| Profiles, dialogs, and shared navigation | Profiles and controls need the same compact, premium visual language. | Retained compact horse facts, source caveats, permanent IDs, and placeholders; refreshed the sidebar, controls, drawers, and dialogs. |

The month grid intentionally includes only full recorded dates whose evidence status is suitable for a schedule view. Held or unresolved dates, partial or missing days, and non-runs remain separately visible. This audit is based on application source and the supplied screenshot; live browser comparison could not pass the current Cloudflare Access email-code screen.
