## Important boundaries

- **Harrowgate:** Margot Kincaid is proposed as its 1999 managing principal. The 1958 founder remains unidentified. Miriam Vale still operates Ashby Vale, Virginia—not Harrowgate, Kentucky.
- **Crescent Mile:** Henry Bellamy is a proposed current leader, NOT a claim about the historic racing-owner ledger for Who Knows or Saint Nobody.
- **Ironweed:** Wade Pritchard is proposed as family principal; Ray Sutter remains a professional trainer connection, not the owner. Circle B Racing is separately run by proposed Tex Colter.
- **Poppy Lane:** Nathan Park is the proposed 1999 managing partner, joined by proposed Evan Mercer and Jonathan Vail. No change to the pre-acquisition 1990–93 stakes credits.
- **Argosy:** Julian Voss remains the established Argosy Bloodstock founder/chief strategist. Helena Roche is the separate proposed Argosy Racing Partners investment manager.
- **Takamori:** Hiroshi Takamori is proposed to run the California division; Akira Takamori is a separate Japan-side director. Kenjiro remains the historical founder, not a new living 1999 owner identity.
- **Buckley:** Charles and Andrew have independent farm and person IDs. Their brotherhood/rivalry is proposed game lore, not evidence of land or horse transfers.
- **Samuels Family Racing:** Edmund Samuels is an entirely fictional WTW character. Do not treat this as evidence of an actual family's Thoroughbred ownership or imply Maker's Mark endorsement.
- **Public figures:** Mike Ditka, Bob Knight, Magic Johnson and Pat Buchanan retain their exact pre-existing scenario person/owner pairs. The game-world horse-racing associations do not establish real-world historical business facts.
- **Aurelian:** David Carlin and Elliot Kessler both remain linked under one stud identity, while existing Argosy rivalry/horse history stays untouched.
